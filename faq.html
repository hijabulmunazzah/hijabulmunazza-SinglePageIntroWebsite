Malaki Silk aur Chiffon Georgette ko wash aur iron kaise karein?

• Washing: Hamesha thande paani mein gentle hand-wash karein ya wash bag mein machine ke delicate cycle par wash karein. Har wash ke baad direct shade mein sukhayein.
• Ironing: Georgette Chiffon aur Silk ko hamesha low-to-medium heat ya steam iron karein taake fabric ki natural shine aur drape mehfooz rahe.
