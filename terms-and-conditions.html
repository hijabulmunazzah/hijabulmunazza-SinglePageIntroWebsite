Terms & Conditions

Guidelines, ordering terms, and customer rights for Hijab ul Munnazah orders across Pakistan.

1. Acceptance of Terms

By placing an order via our website or official WhatsApp channel (+92 307 1771829), you agree to the terms and conditions outlined below.

2. Open Parcel Inspection (Tashaffi Guarantee)

All deliveries across Pakistan come with our Open Parcel Inspection guarantee. You are entitled to open and verify the parcel contents in front of the courier rider before handing over cash payment. If the item does not match your ordered shade or fabric, you may refuse acceptance on the spot without penalty.

3. Pricing & Wholesale Structure

All prices are quoted in Pakistani Rupees (PKR). As direct textile manufacturers, our catalog prices reflect factory discount pricing (20% to 40% below typical mall boutique rates). Wholesale pricing is activated for orders of 12 pieces or more.

4. 7-Day Exchange Policy

If you wish to exchange a color or fabric after receiving your parcel, you may notify us via WhatsApp within 7 calendar days of delivery. Items must remain unwashed, unworn, and in original packaging.

5. Delivery Timeline

Orders within Karachi are normally delivered in 24 to 48 hours. Orders across other major cities (Lahore, Islamabad, Rawalpindi, Faisalabad, Multan, Peshawar, Quetta, etc.) typically take 2 to 4 business days. Free shipping applies automatically to all orders exceeding Rs. 2,500.

6. Wholesale Orders & Bulk Agreements

For bulk custom dyes, madrasa uniforms, or private labeling contracts, delivery schedules and deposit terms are confirmed via mutual agreement through our official email (hijabulmunazzah@gmail.com) or WhatsApp desk.
